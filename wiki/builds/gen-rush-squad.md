# Gen Rush Squad

## Perks
1. [[prove-thyself|Prove Thyself]] — faster gens when co-oping
2. [[windows-of-opportunity|Windows of Opportunity]] — win the chase when it happens
3. [[bond|Bond]] — know where your team is without voice comms
4. [[adrenaline|Adrenaline]] — free heal + haste burst when the last gen pops

## Why it works
All four perks are individually among the most-used survivor perks in the game (see each perk page for pick-rate data) — this build is less a hidden tech and more "what most of the playerbase is already running, assembled on purpose." The core idea: finish generators before the killer can apply real pressure, and use Adrenaline to bail out a bad chase at the very end.

## Notes
This is the closest thing to a "default meta" survivor loadout right now, built entirely from perks with confirmed real pick-rate data (7.1.0 stats window, see [[trivia/jolt-was-surge|perk stats source]] pages) rather than a name I invented.
