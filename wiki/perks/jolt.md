# Jolt

## Effect
Downing a survivor instantly regresses every generator within 32 meters — no need to walk to a gen and kick it.

## Why it's used
It's a passive, hands-off gen-defense perk that triggers off something killers do constantly anyway (downing survivors). No setup, no tracking required — a big part of why it's the single most-used killer perk right now.

## Trivia
See [[jolt-was-surge|Jolt Used to Be Called Surge]] — it started life as The Demogorgon's unique perk before the Stranger Things license lapsed.
