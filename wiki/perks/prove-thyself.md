# Prove Thyself

## Effect
Rewards co-oping generators instead of splitting up — the more survivors on one gen, the faster it goes.

## Why it's used
Core piece of any gen-rush strategy: pressures the killer by finishing generators before they can apply pressure to the team. See [[gen-rush-squad|Gen Rush Squad]] build.
