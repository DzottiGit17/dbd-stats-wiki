# Adrenaline

## Effect
Free heal + speed burst the moment the last generator pops — often turns a losing chase into an escape.

## Why it's used
A single-perk "get out of jail free" for the endgame, tied to Meg Thomas. Strong specifically because gen-rush strategies (see survivor builds) make the exit-gate trigger come up reliably.
