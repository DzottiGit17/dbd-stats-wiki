# Lethal Pursuer

## Effect
Reveals every survivor's aura for a few extra seconds at match start, so the killer knows exactly where everyone spawned.

## Why it's used
Removes the "blind" opening minute of a trial entirely — pairs naturally with Corrupt Intervention for a strong early-game combo (see [[gen-defense-opener|Gen Defense Opener]] build).
