# Barbecue & Chilli

## Effect
After hooking a survivor, reveals auras of other survivors who are far from the hook — helps decide where to go next instead of camping.

## Why it's used
Rewards spreading pressure across the map instead of tunneling one hook, and it's been a staple "information" perk since launch. Originally The Cannibal's (Bubba Sawyer) unique perk, now teachable to everyone.
