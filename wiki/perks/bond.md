# Bond

## Effect
Constant aura-read on nearby teammates — know where they are and what they're doing without comms.

## Why it's used
The default "team awareness" perk, especially for solo queue where there's no voice chat to coordinate gen spreading or know who needs a save. Dwight Fairfield's signature perk.
