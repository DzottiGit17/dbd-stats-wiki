# Corrupt Intervention

## Effect
Blocks the 3 farthest generators for up to 2 minutes at the start of the match, funneling survivors toward a smaller area.

## Why it's used
Buys the killer early map control before survivors can spread out across every generator — especially strong paired with perks that punish survivors for grouping up.
