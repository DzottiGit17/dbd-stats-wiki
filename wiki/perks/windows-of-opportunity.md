# Windows of Opportunity

## Effect
Always shows nearby pallets and breakable walls — huge for learning map layouts and chase routes.

## Why it's used
The single most-used survivor perk in the game (29% pick rate). It's a pure information perk with no downside, and it directly helps newer players learn loop tanking/pallet spacing, which is why it sees use at every skill level.
