# The Deathslinger

## Power
Fires a harpoon that hooks a survivor and reels them in — a ranged killer, but slower and more punishable than The Huntress if the shot misses.

## Trivia
Reported as the **3rd most popular killer in early 2024**, called "a surprising entry" in coverage at the time given he's considered easy to pick up but effective once aim is learned.
