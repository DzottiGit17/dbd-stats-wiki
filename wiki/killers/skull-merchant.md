# Skull Merchant

## Power
Deploys tracking drones that scan nearby survivors, applying stacking "Haemorrhage/Exposed"-style marks that punish survivors for lingering in scanned zones.

## Playstyle notes
Strong area-denial/zoning killer rather than a chase specialist — leans on drone placement and information over raw chase power.

## Trivia
Ranks among the top killers by **kill rate** per community-tracked stats — see [[deadliest-killers-kill-rate|Deadliest Killers by Kill Rate]]. Kill rate ≠ popularity: she's controversial in the community precisely because a strong kill rate against average survivors doesn't reflect how she performs against coordinated/high-MMR teams.
