# The Hag

## Power
Plants invisible traps around the map/hooks; triggering one lets her teleport there instantly — a trap-and-ambush playstyle rather than direct chasing.

## Trivia
Named one of the **least popular killers** as of November 2024 (alongside Skull Merchant), per NightLight-sourced stats covering ~21,924 games from Oct 21–Nov 18, 2024. Her low pick rate is often attributed to a stationary, trap-reliant playstyle that many players find less engaging than mobile killers.
