# The Huntress

## Power
Throws hatchets that down survivors on hit from range — the only classic ranged-attack killer for most of the game's history.

## Playstyle notes
Rewards aim skill over chase mechanics; strong at long sightlines, weaker in tight indoor loops where hatchets can't be thrown cleanly.

## Trivia
Per late-2024 stats coverage, The Huntress was the **most popular killer** with a 5.7% pick rate and a 54.22% kill rate — notable since she's one of the oldest killers in the game (Chapter 4) still topping pick-rate charts years later.
