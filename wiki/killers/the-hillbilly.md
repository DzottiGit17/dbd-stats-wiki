# The Hillbilly

## Power
Revs a chainsaw for a fast, instant-down charge attack — one of the original base-game killers, focused entirely on chainsaw-sprint mastery.

## Trivia
Was reported as the **most popular killer in early 2024** with an 8% pick rate, following a rework that significantly improved his chainsaw handling — a notable case of a balance patch reviving a long-underused base-game killer's popularity.
