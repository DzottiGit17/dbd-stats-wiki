# Average Kill Rate: ~58.5%

BHVR's internal telemetry, shared in a developer update, showed killers eliminating **58.5% of survivors on average** as of data through February 2024. This follows an earlier balance push after the June 2022 (6.0.0) update, where BHVR explicitly aimed to raise the average kill rate toward 60%.

BHVR has repeatedly said in these updates that **kill rate alone isn't the full balance picture** — pick rate, perk usage, and player skill distribution (MMR) all factor into how they read the number.

## Notes
This is genuinely BHVR's own reported figure (`confidence: official`), not a community estimate — though it's specific to the data window it was reported for and will drift patch to patch.
