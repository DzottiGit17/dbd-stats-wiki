# Deadliest Killers by Kill Rate

Per community-aggregated match data (NightLight.gg pulls from thousands of uploaded matches, not BHVR's own telemetry), the killers with the highest kill rates are:

1. **Skull Merchant**
2. **The Plague**
3. **Sadako**

High kill rate doesn't necessarily mean "most fun" or "most played" — it often correlates with killers that are strong against average-skill survivors but heavily countered by coordinated/high-MMR groups. See [[deadliest-killers-kill-rate|this page]] alongside [[most-played-survivors|Most Played Survivors]] for the pick-rate vs. kill-rate contrast.
