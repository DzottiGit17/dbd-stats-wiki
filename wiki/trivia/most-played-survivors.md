# Most-Played Survivors

BHVR's own developer stats reveal put the most-played survivor ranking as:

1. **Claudette Morel**
2. **Meg Thomas**
3. **Feng Min**

Claudette being #1 tracks with community folklore — her perks (Self-Care, Botany Knowledge) and low-key cosmetic pool have made her a long-running "default pick" for a lot of the playerbase.
