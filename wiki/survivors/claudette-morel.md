# Claudette Morel

## Background
One of the four original survivors from the base game. Botanist/healer archetype — her kit revolves entirely around healing efficiency.

## Trivia
Per BHVR's own stats reveal, Claudette is the **most-played survivor** in the game — see [[most-played-survivors|Most Played Survivors]]. Community folklore attributes this partly to her strong self-sufficient perk kit (Self-Care especially) making her a reliable pick regardless of teammates, and partly to a long-running meme about her camouflaging into dark corners of maps.

## Common perks used
- Self-Care (own signature perk)
- Windows of Opportunity (see [[windows-of-opportunity|Windows of Opportunity]] — most-used survivor perk overall, not Claudette-specific)
